Idhu video-la irukkura technology semma futuristic-ah irukkula.

Aana interesting part enna-na, idhu just oru Tesla maathiri-aana Autopilot car kedayadhu, ena most of the Tesla systems still require an attentive human driver.

*Aana indha car-la driver badhila, cameras, LiDAR, radar, GPS and AI irukku. Road-la enna nadakkudhu-nu continuously sense panni, AI decision eduthu steering, braking, acceleration ellathayum control pannudhu.*

Adhu mattum illaama, munnadi oru vehicle sudden-ah brake potta, pedestrian cross panna vandha, illa road-la oru obstacle irundha, adha detect panni situation-ku yetha maadhiri speed-ah reduce panni, thevai-na stop panni, safe-ah continue pannum.

*China-la idha just experiment-aa vechikala. Baidu-oda Apollo Go maadhiri companies designated areas-la fully driverless robotaxi services-ah already operate pannitu irukkaanga. US-la Waymo and Dubai maadhiri places-layum robotaxi expansion nadakkudhu.*

Oru taxi 24/7 operate panna mudinja, driver dependency reduce aagum. Same fleet-la more rides complete panna mudiyum. So labour cost mattum illaama, fleet utilisation-um productivity-um improve aagum.

Appo India-ku idhu varuma nu ketingana?

*Inga technology mattum problem illa. India-oda unpredictable traffic, pedestrians, two-wheelers, road conditions, regulations and safety responsibility ellame big challenges.*

Adhanaala dhaan Tesla-kooda India-la launch pannina models-la full autonomous driving-ah immediately offer pannala.

*But future-la, airports, tech parks, gated areas maadhiri controlled places-la, indha cars-ku dedicated routes or road structure create panni operate panna mudiyum. Adhu successful-a irundha, gradually innum pala areas-kku expand panna chances irukku.*

So, indha technology India-ku vandha, unga opinion enna? Idhu useful-ah irukkuma, illa risky-ah irukkuma? Comments-la sollunga.
